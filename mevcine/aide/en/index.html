MeVCine

Measuring an object's position from video frames

Open a video you have recorded or received in class. MeVCine associates each point you mark on the object with a frame and a time. With a coordinate system and a known length, you can read positions in metres, examine the trajectory and analyse the data.

Make a first set of measurements

For a first measurement, choose a short video showing a ball and a ruler in the same shot. Open it in simple mode.

  1. Place the origin at a fixed landmark. Choose the direction of the axis you will use to describe the motion.
  2. Join two graduations a known distance apart. Enter the distance and its unit.
  3. Go to the first frame of interest. Mark the centre of the ball in three consecutive frames.
  4. Read the three times and positions in the table. Play through the frames to check the direction in which the coordinate changes.
  5. Save your project. For a first graph, switch to expert mode and plot y against t.

All five screens are illustrated in Simple mode. Teachers can use these measurements in the suggested practical.

Read the marking window

The instructions and magnifier are on the left, the video is in the centre and the table is on the right. The status bar at the bottom shows the current frame and the next action. Click any screenshot in this guide to enlarge it.

Choose a working mode

Simple mode guides you through marking. Switch to expert mode to track several objects, change reference frame or plot one quantity against another. Switching modes preserves the objects, times, coordinate system, scale and marks.

The table gives times and positions. The grapher can also calculate velocity and acceleration from three successive positions. Use Python or CSV export to analyse the measurements yourself.
