Frequently asked questions

The small problems of the lab, and how to solve them

Marking

I click on the object, and nothing happens.
In simple mode, marking happens only at step 4 (or to redo a measurement with ⌖). In expert mode, first Start marking, and the frame must follow those already marked: no gaps are allowed. Also check that drawing the reference or choosing the origin is not in progress: the status bar says so; Esc cancels it.
I clicked next to the object.
Right after: ↶ Undo the last point (Ctrl+Z). Later: the ⌖ button of the row, in the table of measurements, then a click on the object.
My points zigzag.
Always aim at the same point of the object (its centre, or its front edge), looking at the magnifier. A blurred object in the video is hard to aim at: film with more light (see Filming a motion).

The scale

My length is refused.
Type a number, and choose the unit in the list: mm, cm or m. Another unit (km, dm…) is not accepted. See Writing the length.
My positions are in pixels (x (px)).
The scale is not set. Set it, even after marking: the measurements are recomputed.
My distances are far too large, or too small.
Check the unit of the reference: 85 in m instead of cm gives distances 100 times too large. Correct the unit, then Confirm again.

Times

The motion looks too slow, the times seem wrong.
The video is probably in slow motion: enter the capture frame rate (for instance 240) in Slow-motion video? (step 4) or Frames per second (expert mode). See In slow motion.
The time intervals in the table are not all equal.
The video has a variable frame rate (phone): MeVCine uses the real time of each frame. That is normal, and more accurate.
Why is the first time 0?
Time zero is that of the first marked frame: you choose the origin of time by choosing where to start marking.

Modes, the window

How do I switch to expert mode, or back to simple mode?
With the Mode menu, at any time. The measurements are kept.
“Switch to simple mode?”: MeVCine wants to keep only object 1.
Simple mode follows only one object. Keep object 1 forgets the others; Cancel stays in expert mode, with all the objects.
The texts are in French. / The screen is too dark, or too light.
The flag, at the top right, changes the language; the sun (or the moon), next to it, changes the theme.
The velocity vectors are too long, or too short.
Trajectories tab: type another value in Velocity scale (pixels for 1 m/s), or empty the box to go back to the automatic scale.

Export

The text pasted into Python gives an error.
Paste all the copied text, including the first line import numpy as np, at the beginning of the program. If numpy is not installed on the computer, ask your teacher.
The spreadsheet shows odd numbers.
The CSV file uses a decimal point: see The CSV file.
I closed MeVCine without saving.
Start it again, then File › Recover previous work…: the automatic backup gives back the video, the axes, the scale and the measurements.
“Video not found” when reopening my project.
The project does not contain the video: put it next to the .mevcine file, then reopen it.

Another message? See Troubleshooting, or ask your teacher.
