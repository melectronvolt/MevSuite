Getting started

Starting MeVCine, opening a video, choosing the mode

Starting MeVCine

Double-click on MeVCine (or on the shortcut prepared by your teacher). A splash screen shows for two seconds; a click makes it disappear at once.

MeVCine starts in the mode used last time (simple mode the first time), and offers at once to open a video.

Opening a video

  • 📂 Open a video…: your own, filmed with a phone or a camera (also File › Open a video…, or Ctrl+O);
  • or drag the video file onto the MeVCine window.

A large video takes a few seconds to open: the busy cursor shows, and the window does not respond until it is done. Please wait.

MeVCine reads videos from phones (iPhone, Android) and cameras, even filmed in portrait or in slow motion. If the video is lying on its side, you can rotate it at step 1. If an “Unreadable video” message appears, ask your teacher, or see Filming a motion.

Choosing the mode

  • Mode › Simple mode (step by step): the six steps, one by one, for a single object. To get started, and for most labs. See Simple mode.
  • Mode › Expert mode (all features): several objects, automatic tracking, trajectories, grapher. See Expert mode.

You can change mode at any time, without losing the measurements (simple mode keeps only object 1). The status bar recalls the mode, bottom right, and MeVCine remembers it for next time.

Resuming a piece of work

  • File › Open a project… reopens saved work (you can also drag the project file onto the window): the video, the axes, the scale and the measurements come back. The video must be next to the project file, or at its original place. If MeVCine is installed on the computer, a double-click on the .mevcine file opens it too.
  • File › Recover previous work… reopens the automatic backup, after an accidental closing or a crash. See Nothing is lost without warning.

MeVCine never reopens the previous student's video by itself: everyone starts from a clean screen.

The menus

At the very top, the menus:

Fileopen a video; open a project, recover previous work, save the project (video, axes, scale, measurements, to continue later); copy for Python, save as CSV; quit
Editundo the last point, redo it, start over
Modesimple mode or expert mode, without losing the measurements
HelpUser guide…: this guide (F1: at the page of what you are doing); About MeVCine…; About Qt…

At the far right, on the same line:

☀ or ☾the theme: light (sun) or dark (moon). A click switches to the other one; your choice is remembered.
Flagthe language of the interface: French or English. A click switches to the other one; it is remembered too.

Most buttons explain what they do when the mouse rests on them for a moment. Texts too small, on the board or the projector? Ctrl++ enlarges the whole interface, Ctrl+− shrinks it, Ctrl+0 brings it back to its normal size.

Quitting

Close the window, or File › Quit (Ctrl+Q). If your measurements have been neither copied nor saved, MeVCine reminds you before closing.
